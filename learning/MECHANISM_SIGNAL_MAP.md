# Mechanism & Signal Map

Status: **LEARNING / TRACEABILITY ONLY**. This map does not change claims, decisions, gates or permitted use.

`Relationship → Observed vs Expected Conditional Response → Continuous Pair-Specific Abnormality → Sequential Mechanism Evidence Update + Dynamic Resolution Prediction → Trade / Update / Reject / Abstain → OOS Validation`

Mechanism and resolution beliefs may update jointly. This is not `hard trigger → mandatory classification → trade`. `U = Unresolved / Abstain` is epistemic, not a fifth economic mechanism.

| State | Current definition | Opportunity / preservation logic | Identification | Signal/model progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 | Positive structural, fundamental or relationship-invalidating evidence | Reject false opportunities; avoid loss; preserve capital | PARTIAL; positive evidence required | G2 PENDING; no model |
| M1 | Peer has not moved sufficiently in its expected signed direction after linked information | Possible later follower catch-up | PARTIAL; lead–lag alone insufficient | Literature REVIEWED; measurement G2 PENDING |
| M2 | Shocked/source asset moved excessively relative to expected conditional response under candidate temporary pressure | Possible later source normalization/reversal | PARTIAL; pressure proxies contaminated/endogenous | Literature REVIEWED; measurement G2 PENDING |
| M3 | Candidate temporary idiosyncratic relative dislocation | Possible later relative normalization | **WEAK / NON-IDENTIFIED** | No positive label permitted |
| U | Evidence insufficient or too ambiguous to assign an economic mechanism | Abstain/no-trade; avoid forced errors | FROZEN epistemic role | Representation G2 PENDING |

Preserved boundaries: opposite-direction movement is neither automatically abnormal nor M3; future catch-up/reversal/normalization is validation-only; relationship validity is not trading predictability; signal concept ≠ candidate measurement ≠ frozen factor ≠ model output ≠ trade rule.

## Frozen G2-02 implication

The Relationship step contains **P0**, the primary simpler PIT market/statistical layer whose five non-equivalent information families remain separate, and optional **P1**, which adds a separately identifiable slow Economic/Company Relationship layer. Neither layer is an abnormality detector, mechanism classifier, resolution model or trade rule. Slow P1 context is normally precomputed/cached; a new company event requires separate PIT treatment and later authorization as discriminator/rejection evidence. P1 requires incremental normal-relationship-level value; downstream prediction or PnL alone is insufficient. Status: **FROZEN SEMANTICS; MEASUREMENT UNRESOLVED**.
