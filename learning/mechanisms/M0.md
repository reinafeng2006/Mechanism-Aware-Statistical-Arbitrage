1. **Definition:** positive structural, fundamental, stale-link, persistent-break or constraint evidence that weakens the arbitrage interpretation. Absence of other mechanisms is insufficient.
2. **Economic intuition:** the apparent anomaly may reflect a changed or invalid normal relationship rather than temporary mispricing.
3. **Opportunity:** reject a false opportunity.
4. **Money preservation:** loss avoidance, capital preservation and reduced exposure to persistent divergence; profitability is not inferred.

8. **PIT evidence:** dated fundamental/company-specific news, relationship/link change, persistent break evidence and lawful funding/constraint context where available.
