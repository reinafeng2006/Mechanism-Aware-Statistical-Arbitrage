# M1 — Delayed Information Incorporation / Limited Attention

1. **Definition:** after identified linked information, the peer has not moved sufficiently in its expected signed direction. `lead-lag ≠ M1 identification`.
2. **Economic intuition:** economically transmitted information may be incorporated into a linked peer with delay.
3. **Opportunity:** possible later follower catch-up.
4. **Monetization logic:** a correctly timed exposure to expected signed follower adjustment could earn returns; no profitability is established.
5. **Known:** source/link/timing, signed under-response and rival checks are supported belief-update components.
6. **Unknown:** final under-response measurement, expected-response/uncertainty estimators, clock, causal attention state, horizon, effect size and A-share transferability.

8. **PIT evidence:** link vintage/direction, first-public source event, source/peer responses through decision time, peer own-news and market/industry context.
9. **Expected resolution — validation only:** future signed follower catch-up; never an event-time input.
10. **Signal Architecture:** P directional relationship + S abnormal response + C source/link/timing/under-response + R rival checks.

## G2-06 candidate measurement architecture

The proposal separates Expected Signed Response, Observed Response, Signed Response Gap, Response Uncertainty and Under-Response Evidence. Positive orientation means insufficient movement in the expected signed direction; overshoot has the opposite orientation. Near-zero or too-uncertain direction remains unoriented/U rather than mechanical M1. A signed gap may be a general abnormality morphology; it becomes an M1-specific candidate interpretation only with PIT source/link/timing and rival evidence. UR0 raw gap, UR1 uncertainty-standardized gap and UR2 context-adjusted gap are competing **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAUTHORIZED** families. UR2 must not condition again on information already used in Expected Signed Response. UR0's transformation may be easy, but end-to-end feasibility inherits the expected-response architecture. No formula, estimator, clock, threshold or M1 rule is selected.
