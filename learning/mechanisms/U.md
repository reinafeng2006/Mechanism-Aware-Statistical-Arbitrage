## Frozen G2-10 representation semantics

`U = epistemic state`; `Abstain = decision/action`; `No Trade = possible downstream trading outcome`. They are not synonyms. U is dynamic and time-indexed: new PIT evidence may support later transitions from U to M0/M1/M2 or continued U, without rewriting the earlier record. `Not yet known` distinguishes an expected information wait from `currently non-identifiable`, where authorized current information cannot distinguish rivals.

Live unexplained abnormality routes to U rather than M3, while positive structural/fundamental evidence is required for M0. U need not cause immediate No Trade, and Abstain may occur without U because of costs, payoff, risk or implementation constraints. Any dedicated representation inherits G2-05 feasibility governance and should preferentially reuse existing uncertainty, quality, timing and provenance metadata.

U0–U4 remain competing **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAUTHORIZED** candidates. No probability simplex, uncertainty metric, threshold, transition model, abstention/waiting policy or trade rule is selected.
