# M2 — Temporary Liquidity / Flow Pressure

1. **Definition:** the shocked/source asset may have moved excessively relative to its expected conditional response because of temporary liquidity/order-flow pressure.
2. **Economic intuition:** non-informational demand or supply can temporarily move price away from conditional normality.
3. **Opportunity:** possible later source normalization or reversal.
4. **Monetization logic:** exposure against temporary over-movement could earn reversal returns; no profitability is established.
5. **Known:** pressure source + liquidity state + abnormal response + rival checks may update belief; generic flow/volume/illiquidity is insufficient.

8. **PIT evidence:** lawful released pressure context, lagged overlap, known calendar/settlement context, contemporaneous price/flow/quote/liquidity response and rival news.
9. **Expected resolution — validation only:** future source reversal/normalization or persistence; never an event-time input.
