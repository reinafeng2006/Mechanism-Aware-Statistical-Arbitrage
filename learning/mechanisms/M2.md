# M2 — Temporary Liquidity / Flow Pressure

1. **Definition:** the shocked/source asset may have moved excessively relative to its expected conditional response because of temporary liquidity/order-flow pressure.
2. **Economic intuition:** non-informational demand or supply can temporarily move price away from conditional normality.
3. **Opportunity:** possible later source normalization or reversal.
4. **Monetization logic:** exposure against temporary over-movement could earn reversal returns; no profitability is established.
5. **Known:** pressure source + liquidity state + abnormal response + rival checks may update belief; generic flow/volume/illiquidity is insufficient.
6. **Unknown:** final proxy, exogeneity, contamination adjudication, excess-response/uncertainty estimator, clock, horizon and A-share transferability.
7. **Competing interpretations:** information repricing, M1, shortage versus oversupply, fundamentals, microstructure effects, M0 and noise.
8. **PIT evidence:** lawful released pressure context, lagged overlap, known calendar/settlement context, contemporaneous price/flow/quote/liquidity response and rival news.
9. **Expected resolution — validation only:** future source reversal/normalization or persistence; never an event-time input.
10. **Signal Architecture:** P conditional relationship + S over-movement + C pressure/liquidity evidence + R contamination/information checks.
11. **Candidate Model Architecture:** **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAUTHORIZED** `P(M2|F_t)` or a belief function; no classifier/filter selected.
12. **Progress:** definition DEFINED; literature REVIEWED/PARTIAL; G2-07 semantics FROZEN with MP0–MP3 candidates UNSELECTED/UNAUTHORIZED; data G3 PENDING; implementation/OOS/economics NOT STARTED.
13. **Ancestry:** D-014/D-017; G1-04; CL-M2-001–004, CL-B4-002; M2-001/002/003/005 and B4-004 Paper Guides; M2 Learning Card.

## G2-07 candidate measurement architecture

The proposal separates Expected Signed Conditional Response, Observed Source Response, Oriented Excess Gap, Response/Relationship Uncertainty, Pressure-Source Evidence, Liquidity/Flow-State Evidence, Proxy-Contamination Audit, Rival Evidence and downstream M2 Evidence. MP0 excess diagnostic, MP1 accessible context, MP2 pressure-source/contamination-audited diagnostic and MP3 difficult high-frequency permanent/transitory extension are competing **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAUTHORIZED** candidates. A proxy must carry a Contamination Record and seven-time PIT lineage. M1 under-response and M2 excess movement concern different assets/evidence chains and are not arithmetic negatives. No proxy, formula, estimator, clock, threshold or M2 rule is selected.
