Saturation is bounded to a named current G1 question, construct/mechanism, population, horizon, and intended downstream decision. It is never a claim that a topic is complete.

Allowed states:

- `OPEN`: a named missing link, contradiction, or transferability issue could change the eventual permitted-use boundary.
- `PROVISIONALLY_SATURATED`: enough reviewed evidence exists for one bounded decision; routine review stops.
- `REOPENED`: a documented trigger invalidates an earlier stopping judgment.

Before retaining `PROVISIONALLY_SATURATED`, assess contradictions, population/market transfer, temporal relevance, horizon mismatch, point-in-time timing, regime/market-structure mismatch, and missing validation links. Reopen only with a recorded trigger, affected claims, target question, owner, and date.
