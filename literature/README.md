# Literature Review Operating System

Status: **MIGRATED PROCESS INFRASTRUCTURE — G1-03 REMAINS UNAUTHORIZED**

This directory contains current-project literature process infrastructure. It is adapted from the predecessor Mean-Reversion-Stationarity repository at the workflow/governance level only. It contains no inherited empirical result, claim, conclusion, method selection, threshold, permitted-use decision, or current-project evidence.

## Governing path inside G1

`Discovery → Triage → Light → Selective Deep → Synthesis Checkpoint`

- **G1-01:** Discovery and Triage.
- **G1-02:** Light and Selective Deep adaptive review. Review depth is decision-triggered, not a paper-count requirement.
- **G1-03:** Cross-Paper Evidence Synthesis. It alone may create the formal Pair Formation Evidence Map, Mechanism Observable-Signature Map, Literature-Derived Data Requirement Map, and Unresolved Gap Map.
- **G1-04:** Evidence Freeze & G2 Handoff. It alone may freeze permitted downstream use.

Current state: G1-01 and G1-02 are complete. G1-03 and G1-04 remain locked.

## Full-text-only admission

`FULL TEXT AVAILABLE != EVIDENCE ADMITTED`  
`PAPER REVIEWED != EVERY PAPER CLAIM ACCEPTED`

Discovery metadata, abstracts, snippets, secondary summaries, Observatory material, and predecessor content can support discovery or process design only. A current substantive claim requires lawful accessible full text, verified identity/version, claim-level provenance, and current-project review under one or more of A-P1–A-P3 or B-P1–B-P4.

## Adaptive review depth

- **Discovery:** identity and possible decision relevance only.
- **Triage:** question mapping, construct, market/sample, full-text status, likely decision value, and proposed depth.
- **Light:** targeted current-project claim extraction with locator, logic chain, limitations, competing explanation, missing link, validation test, project-testability, and use boundary.
- **Selective Deep:** only when a paper can materially change a current G1 decision, contradiction interpretation, dependency, permitted-use boundary, or G2 measurement candidate. It receives a dedicated review file under `reviews/`.

Stop review when additional work is unlikely to change a permitted-use boundary, missing-link assessment, validation-test requirement, or downstream measurement choice. A credible contradiction, changed object/population/horizon/regime, newly material missing link, or failed later validation may reopen a bounded question.

## Directory responsibilities

- `library/`: user-managed full texts only; ignored by Git; never modified, moved, renamed, or deleted by the research process.
- `bibliography.md`: paper-level inventory and triage metadata; it contains no claim credibility decision.
- `claim_schema.md`: current-project claim/edge schema and field meanings.
- `evidence_saturation.md`: bounded decision-saturation states and reopen triggers.
- `contradiction_dependency_register.md`: relationships between current claim IDs only.
- `reconciliation_g1_02.md`: one-to-one migration reconciliation of the 15 existing claims and 15 provisional Data Requirements.
- `reviews/`: Selective Deep review files only.
- `validators/`: deterministic checks of ID lineage, reconciliation, boundary status, and prohibited predecessor import.

## Separation and quarantine

The predecessor repository is a process source only. Its historical Paper IDs, Claim IDs, papers, empirical results, conclusions, applicability decisions, model choices, thresholds, permitted-use decisions, and artifacts are quarantined. A paper previously reviewed there must be independently inventoried, fully accessed, mapped to a current G1 question, and re-admitted before it can appear as current evidence.

Current literature evidence lives in `registers/LITERATURE_EVIDENCE.md`; current provisional data needs live in `registers/DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md`. This OS governs them without altering their substantive content or provisional status.
