# M1-008 — Inoue & Todo (2024 working paper)

## Current G1 question and trigger

B-P1: assess whether supply-chain news timing identifies delayed public information incorporation.

## Full-text admission

*Market Reaction to News Flows in Supply Chain Networks*, RIETI Discussion Paper 24-E-077, full text: https://www.rieti.go.jp/jp/publications/dp/24e077.pdf. Reviewed 2026-09-04; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Evidence and identification boundary

The study combines supply-chain links, English-language news sentiment and daily closing prices. Direction follows upstream/downstream link hypotheses: positive client news is expected to raise supplier value through demand; positive supplier news may raise client value through productivity. It compares pre- and post-disclosure windows and expressly recognizes private-channel diffusion and endogeneity before public news release.

CL-M1-003 is **confirmed but materially qualified**. Timestamped public news plus directional supply-chain link provides a stronger candidate source-and-route observable than leader-lag alone. It does not establish that a peer delay after public time is attention: information may already have travelled privately, link databases can be backfilled, news sentiment need not identify the relevant cash-flow shock, and peer responses differ across Japan/global samples.

## Timing, variables, and permitted use

Candidate decision-time observables: PIT link/vintage/direction; first-public news timestamp; source identity and sentiment/content; source and peer returns through the decision timestamp; market context. The paper's daily close convention is coarse for intraday classification. Pre-news response is a rejection/ambiguity signal, not evidence of a clean public-information delay. Future peer response is validation only.

Permitted G2 candidate use: C/R event-time provenance, direction, public timestamp and pre-release-leakage/ambiguity checks. Prohibited: treating news sentiment or post-event follower movement as M1 label, importing NLP model/specification, or assuming private diffusion is absent.

## Missing link / validation / testability

No individual causal M1 label, A-share transfer, or definitive separation of public diffusion from private information is established. G2 needs first-public versus effective-information timing and abstention rules; G4 must test the added explanatory value without future leakage. Requires G3 timestamped news/link vintages and returns; no provider or frequency is selected.
