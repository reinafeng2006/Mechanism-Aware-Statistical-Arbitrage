# M2-003 — Etula et al. (2020)

**Depth:** SELECTIVE DEEP. **Question:** can a predictable institutional cash need strengthen exogeneity?

## Full-text admission

*Dash for Cash: Monthly Market Impact of Institutional Liquidity Needs*, RFS 33, 75–111. Open article: https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/1/75/5494694. Reviewed 2026-09-04.

## Decision-relevant extraction

The source is a recurring payment/settlement cycle that creates predictable month-end institutional cash needs. Settlement rules and calendar dates are observable ex ante; trade-level evidence is used to connect institutions to liquidity-motivated trading. The paper documents price/yield patterns and later reversal around the cycle, but later reversal remains validation only.

This is stronger, narrow exogeneity evidence than generic flow. PIT candidates are calendar/settlement convention, legally observable institutional cash-need context where available, price/volume/liquidity response through decision time, and rival-news checks. It does not identify arbitrary firm-level pressure or transfer its settlement timing to A shares.
