# PF-005 — Carrasco Blázquez, de la Orden de la Cruz & Prado Román (2018)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2. Identify the distinct objects represented by distance, correlation, stochastic/residual, and cointegration approaches; test whether the paper justifies a general representation winner.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

Open-access article, *European Research on Management and Business Economics* 24(3), 160–167, DOI 10.1016/j.iedeen.2018.05.002. Full-text source: https://www.elsevier.es/index.php?p=revista&pRevista=pdf-simple&pii=S2444883418300457&r=489. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

The study compares five strategy families in one US banking sample (2008–2013): correlation (return correlation plus price ratio), distance (normalized-price deviation), stochastic (spread as an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck-type process), stochastic differential residual, and cointegration (lower-order integrated linear combination). These methods do not merely calculate interchangeable scores: they use different state objects and equilibrium assumptions.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

The paper provides a **Light, qualified comparison**, not a general representation choice. Its conclusion that cointegration is more efficient is based on its sample, implementation and profitability/residual-series objective. It does not identify economic proximity, outcome-independent pair validity, relationship stability, or cross-market generalization. Therefore the result **contradicts any attempt to declare a universal literature winner**, while supporting separate G2 candidate families.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: distinct representations can generate different residual series and candidate pairs. Measurement: the five methods above. Observed evidence: methods have related but distinct selection/residual constructions in the authors' bank-sector exercise. Inference: raw dependence, conditional residuals, and long-run equilibrium are non-equivalent constructs. Final claim: retain multiple representations as G2 comparators; do not inherit the paper's performance ranking.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: an outcome-independent, PIT comparison of representation validity in company-aware A-share pairs. G2 must define each candidate object without collapsing them into a score; G4 must compare them under frozen data/split/multiplicity protocol. Testable only after G2/G3 authorization.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: taxonomy of relationship representations and evidence against assuming they are equivalent. Prohibited: cointegration selection, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck assumption, price-ratio rule, profitability ranking, or a universal-method conclusion.
