# PF-004 — Gatev, Goetzmann & Rouwenhorst (2006)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2 and A-P3. Establish what raw normalized-price distance measures and whether its formation/trading separation supplies outcome-independent generalization evidence.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

Yale ICF Working Paper 08-03, February 2006, full text: https://depot.som.yale.edu/icf/papers/fileuploads/2573/original/08-03.pdf. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

Pairs are chosen by minimum squared distance between normalized cum-dividend price paths over a historical formation period; trading is in a subsequent period. This is raw price-path similarity, not factor-adjusted residual dependence, economic similarity, or cointegration proof. Role: P/S baseline.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

The full text **confirms and narrows** CL-PF-002. It demonstrates a reproducible historical formation-to-subsequent-trading separation and explicitly discusses data-snooping. It does not provide outcome-independent pair validity: its empirical conclusions depend substantially on later returns/profits, and the authors themselves note spurious correlation and restrictive co-integration interpretations. A holdout performance period is not an economic-relationship validation criterion.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: normalized price histories can represent historical relative paths. Measurement: pairwise sum of squared path deviations. Observed evidence: the paper documents a deterministic, historical-only selection workflow. Inference: raw-distance is a required comparator/baseline. Final claim: it is admissible only as one candidate raw relationship object with formation/trading separation; not as an economic-pair or mechanism claim.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: direct outcome-independent comparison of distance with residual and stability constructs. G2 needs a frozen PIT normalization, corporate-action treatment, and non-outcome validity diagnostics; G4 needs a split that prevents outcome-informed selection. Testable only once PIT price/universe data are authorized.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: raw co-movement comparator and formation/trading temporal-separation precedent. Prohibited: selecting it as the final representation, interpreting distance as cointegration, or using profitability/reversion as the pair-validity label.
