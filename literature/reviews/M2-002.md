# M2-002 — Wardlaw (2020)

**Depth:** SELECTIVE DEEP; linked contradiction/identification block with M2-001.

## Full-text admission

*Measuring Mutual Fund Flow Pressure as Shock to Stock Returns*, Journal of Finance 75, 3221–3243. Full-text publisher page: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12962. Reviewed 2026-09-04.

## Decision-relevant extraction

Wardlaw decomposes the standard outflow-pressure construction and shows it directly embeds a stock's realized outflow-quarter return. Once direct return components are removed, the reported quarterly decline is small and subsequent reversal disappears. Thus the familiar flow-pressure proxy is contaminated/endogenous for causal pressure inference.

The paper is not evidence that temporary pressure never occurs. It is a binding identification critique: fund flows, lagged holdings and realized returns cannot be mechanically combined and then treated as an exogenous M2 signal. Corrected flow context may at most motivate a candidate source check after an explicit contamination audit. Quarterly reporting is generally too delayed for an unqualified event-time input.

## Reconciliation and permitted use

CL-M2-002 is **confirmed and strengthens the contradiction boundary**. G2 candidate use: mandatory proxy-decomposition/return-embedding rejection test. Prohibited: standard flow-pressure proxy, generic flow as exogenous pressure, or reversal as contemporaneous evidence.
