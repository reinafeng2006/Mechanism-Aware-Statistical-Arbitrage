# PF-001 — Hoberg & Phillips (2016)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P1 and A-P3. Decide the legitimate scope of company/economic representation in a non-manual, PIT pair-formation system.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

NBER Working Paper 15991 (2010, revised 2012), full text: https://www.nber.org/papers/w15991.pdf. Published version: *Journal of Political Economy* 124(5), 2016. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

The paper applies text analysis to annual 10-K product descriptions to create firm-specific, time-varying product-market similarity and competitor sets. It validates the construct against management discussion of competition, manager-identified rivals, and changes after industry shocks. Role: P, with R relevance where product language changes.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

It identifies a product-market proximity representation—not payoff equivalence, return co-movement, causal price linkage, or an investable pair. The original CL-PF-001 is **confirmed but narrowed**: manager-rival and competition validation supports economic-company similarity as a candidate P-layer prior/constraint, not a statistical normal-relationship proof. Filing text can be strategic, stale, missing, revised, or non-comparable across China/US disclosure regimes.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: product-description language contains economically relevant overlap. Measurement: cleaned annual product text is converted into firm-pair similarity. Observed evidence: its competitor sets align better than fixed industry codes with several competition-related validation targets. Inference: data-driven company similarity can enter the information family for pair formation. Final claim: it may constrain or prioritize a reproducible candidate universe only after PIT A-share validation; it cannot select a pair alone.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: whether versioned Chinese company disclosures predict stable pair relationships independently of later relative returns. G2 must define a PIT text-vintage and company-identity construct; G4 must evaluate its pair-validity contribution separately from monetization. Testable only after G3 provides lawful, timestamped filing vintages. No representation, embedding, similarity formula, or threshold is selected.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted candidate use: company/economic P prior and drift/rejection context. Prohibited: manual pairing, treating text similarity as co-movement, treating annual text as contemporaneous mechanism evidence, or using outcomes to choose the text rule.
