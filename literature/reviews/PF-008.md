# PF-008 — Leung & Tam (2021)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2 and A-P3. Clarify what peer-replication residuals measure versus raw pair relationships.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

arXiv:2103.09987v1, March 2021, full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.09987. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

For each month-end, the authors use twelve months of daily returns and elastic-net projection on all other stocks to make a sparse replicate portfolio; projection R-squared measures replicability and own-minus-replicate return is a portfolio residual. Role: P/S/R candidate information.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

CL-PF-003 is **confirmed but materially narrowed**. The paper supports a fully data-driven, trailing return-based replicate/residual object under a linear-factor framework. It is not a pairwise relation: its peer set can contain multiple securities, its interpretation depends on factor/replication assumptions and regularization, and most empirical support is cross-sectional return evidence. It does not establish company relatedness, stability, pair generalization, or residual superiority.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: if assets share factor exposures, a portfolio of others can replicate an asset. Measurement: elastic-net return projection and R-squared. Observed evidence: theorem conditional on factor assumptions plus historical US implementation. Inference: conditional replicability and raw pair distance measure different objects. Final claim: residual/replicate relation is an admissible comparator family, not a selected pair method.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: whether a sparse replicate portfolio can be reduced to stable company-aware pairs, and whether residual validity is outcome-independent. G2 must keep pairwise and portfolio-replication objects separate and define PIT universe/regularization governance; G4 must compare them without selecting on resolution. Requires G3 PIT returns and universe history.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: candidate factor-adjusted/replication comparator and conditional-replicability diagnostic. Prohibited: adopting elastic net, weights, twelve-month window, SAR/SARP interpretation, or treating R-squared as economic similarity.
