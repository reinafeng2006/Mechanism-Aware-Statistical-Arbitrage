# PF-006 — Investigating the Features of Pairs Trading: A Network Perspective on the Chinese Stock Market (2018)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2 and A-P3. Determine whether relationship persistence can serve as pair-validity/rejection information in Chinese equity context.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

Physica A 505 (2018), DOI 10.1016/j.physa.2018.04.021; full-text article record: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378437118304503. Reviewed 2026-09-03 against the full-text record and article methodology/results; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

The paper forms cointegration networks from daily Chinese stock prices around MTSS, then examines edge survival/reoccurrence across periods. It reports that few links survive and that more than 45% are excluded from reoccurrence circles within twelve months. Role: P/R.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

CL-PF-004 is **confirmed but qualified**. The study identifies historical persistence/fragility of a particular cointegration-network edge definition; it does not identify a causal break, a universal stability window, or ex-ante validity of an individual pair. Same-industry/MTSS associations are descriptive and selection/universe/regime dependent. Thus stability is candidate rejection information, not a proof that a surviving relation is economic or tradable.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: a normal statistical relationship should persist sufficiently to be meaningful. Measurement: repeated trailing cointegration-network edges and recurrence. Observed evidence: many Chinese edges fail to recur. Inference: absence of persistence weakens a pair interpretation. Final claim: a no-valid-pair/rejection branch is literature-supported; exact test/window remains unresolved.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: a purely PIT, outcome-independent stability rule that generalizes to A-share machinery and separates true structural divergence from transient estimation noise. G2 must define stability construct(s) and detection lag; G4 must validate rejection utility without using subsequent trade results for event-time validity. Requires G3 PIT price/universe lineage.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: candidate relationship-persistence/rejection information. Prohibited: using recurrence as a final pair score, inferring M0 cause from any failed edge, or choosing cointegration as final representation.
