# M1-001 — Cohen & Frazzini (2008)

## Current G1 question and trigger

B-P1: what is required to interpret a delayed peer response as limited-attention information incorporation rather than generic lead-lag?

## Full-text admission

*Economic Links and Predictable Returns*, Journal of Finance 63(4), 2008; full text: https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf. Reviewed 2026-09-04; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Evidence and identification boundary

The paper uses material, disclosed customer–supplier links and directions (customer shock → supplier cash-flow/value implication). It explicitly requires that overlooked information be public before prices evolve and sufficiently salient that investors should gather it. It uses lagged linked-firm returns and linked-firm news, controls for supplier own momentum, industry and cross-industry momentum, and examines common fund ownership/trading as an inattention-related cross-sectional test.

This **confirms but narrows** CL-M1-001. A contemporaneous candidate-M1 interpretation requires all of: a PIT-valid directional economic link; an identifiable, timestamped source public event/response; a pre-specified transmission direction consistent with the link; and evidence that the peer has not already incorporated the source information. Lagged linked return alone is not sufficient. Common-fund ownership is supportive research evidence, but its reporting delay makes it unsuitable as an unqualified event-time M1 input.

## Timing, rival explanations, and permitted use

PIT observables: disclosure/effective timestamp and vintage of link; source event/news first-public timestamp and source response; peer price/return through the decision time; contemporaneous own-news and market/industry context. Future peer adjustment, eventual catch-up, portfolio return, and later real performance are validation outcomes only. Rivals include common shocks, supplier own news, industry momentum, stale/misclassified links, risk, liquidity and private information.

Permitted G2 candidate use: P/C link-direction/source-event/timing/rejection constructs. Prohibited: generic customer return as an M1 label, manual links, using future follower return, or inheriting the paper's monthly horizon/strategy.

## Missing link / validation / testability

The paper does not give an individual-event causal attention label or A-share transfer validation. G2 must define link-vintage, source-event and peer-underresponse constructs with a strict timing DAG; G4 must separately test rivals and future resolution. Requires G3 PIT relationship disclosures/news/returns; no data acquisition or rule is selected.
