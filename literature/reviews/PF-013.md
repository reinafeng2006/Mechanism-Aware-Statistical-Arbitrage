# PF-013 — Du, Lan, Luo & Zhong (2018/2019)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2 and A-P3. Assess whether factor-adjusted residual correlations and FDR control provide outcome-independent pair-construction evidence.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

*Factor-adjusted multiple testing of correlations*, 2018 online / 2019 journal version, article record: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167947318301397. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

The paper estimates common factors by PCA, computes factor-adjusted residual correlations, and applies a two-stage factor-adjusted correlation (FACL) multiple-testing procedure controlling false discoveries. It includes theory/simulation and a China HS300 daily-return illustration. Role: P/R.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

CL-PF-006 is **confirmed and narrowed**. Under its approximate-factor/multiplicity assumptions, the procedure supports a statistical false-discovery control for residual-correlation links independent of later trading performance. It does not identify economic peerhood, persistent pair validity, structural stability, causal mechanism, or a winner between raw and residual representations.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: common factors and multiple pair tests can make raw correlations misleading. Measurement: PCA factor adjustment plus FACL/FDR testing. Observed evidence: theoretical error-control results, simulation, and HS300 illustration. Inference: multiplicity control is a candidate validity diagnostic. Final claim: it may be considered at G2 as a statistical-screening/rejection family only, contingent on PIT trailing estimation.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: outcome-independent link between statistically significant residual dependence and economically stable/generalizable company pairs. G2 must specify a comparator, universe, and claim-validity construct; G4 must pre-register multiplicity and temporal split controls. Requires G3 PIT returns/universe; no factor count, PCA rule, or FDR level is selected.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: candidate residual-dependence and false-pair/multiplicity diagnostic. Prohibited: residual-correlation-as-economic-similarity, final pair selection rule, factor/PCA choice, or use of subsequent returns to endorse the relation.
