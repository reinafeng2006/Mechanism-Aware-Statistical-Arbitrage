# B4-004 — Yueshen (2020)

**Depth:** SELECTIVE DEEP. **Question:** what does permanent/transitory decomposition add contemporaneously?

## Full-text admission

*What Is in High-Frequency Price Pressure?* manuscript: https://www.sfm.url.tw/pdf/pastawardpapers/2020-02.pdf. Reviewed 2026-09-04.

## Decision-relevant extraction

Timestamped trades, quotes and signed flow enter a specified state-space microstructure decomposition of permanent information effects and transitory pressure. This can create a narrow C/R diagnostic when inputs and estimation are strictly PIT. Model estimates can be revised by later observations; using future intervals would violate event-time use.

The decomposition is model conditional and does not identify trader motive, forced flow, M1/M2/M3/M0, or a unified classifier. Informed flow, trade-sign error, stale quotes and market design remain rivals.

## Reconciliation and permitted use

CL-B4-002 is **reconfirmed**. G2 may retain a narrow permanent/transitory C/R candidate and strict intraday lineage requirement. No state-space model, trade-sign method or frequency is selected.
