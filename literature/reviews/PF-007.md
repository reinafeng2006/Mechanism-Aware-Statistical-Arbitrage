# PF-007 — Lu, Lai & Shih (2021)

## Current G1 question(s) and decision trigger

A-P2 and B-P3. Determine what structural-break evidence adds to pair validity/rejection without importing the paper's trading algorithm.

## Full-text admission and version/provenance

*Structural break-aware pairs trading strategy using deep reinforcement learning*, 2021, PMC full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8369334/. Reviewed 2026-09-03; citation and researcher-authored notes retained only.

## Construct / method / relevance

For preselected cointegrated Taiwan pairs, the paper uses prior price sequences and time/frequency features to estimate structural-break probability, then couples it to a trading policy. Role: R primarily; P only as a precondition.

## Identification, limitations, and claim reconciliation

CL-PF-005 is **narrowed**. The article supports the conceptual separation of relationship-break detection from a trading policy and illustrates an online-oriented break-probability approach. It does not establish that its detector identifies fundamental structural divergence rather than regime noise, nor does it establish an outcome-independent universal pair-validity rule. Its supervised/performance comparisons and reinforcement-learning policy are outside permitted use.

## Logic chain and downstream boundary

Premise: a previously estimated cointegrated relation can cease to hold. Measurement: model-specific features and break probability. Observed evidence: the paper reports detector/policy performance on Taiwan tick data. Inference: break risk deserves a dedicated rejection information family. Final claim: G2 may define a break/rejection construct, but must not inherit this method, policy, threshold, or performance claim.

## Missing link, validation test, project testability

Missing link: causal correspondence between detected breaks and fundamental/M0 divergence, under strict PIT timing. G2 requires a timing DAG and an algorithm-independent rejection construct; G4 requires precommitted false-alarm/delay criteria without outcome leakage. Testable only with authorized PIT data.

## Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: candidate R-layer break monitoring and explicit no-valid-pair path. Prohibited: deep-Q/RL algorithm selection, tick-frequency commitment, break-probability threshold, or M0 labeling.
