# M2-005 — Kang, Lin & Xiong (2022)

**Depth:** SELECTIVE DEEP. **Question:** are illiquidity and liquidity oversupply interchangeable explanations?

## Full-text admission

*What Drives Intraday Reversal? Illiquidity or Liquidity Oversupply?*, Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control 136 (2022), full article text at https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165188922000185. Reviewed 2026-09-04.

## Decision-relevant extraction

The paper contrasts a temporary price concession from scarce liquidity with a pricing error from excessive liquidity supplied by uninformed traders. It uses intraday prior/subsequent returns, size, turnover and Amihud illiquidity in China/US comparisons. In its China setting, reversal is weakly conditioned by illiquidity and later "reversal of reversal" is interpreted as more consistent with liquidity oversupply.

Neither abnormal volume/turnover nor illiquidity identifies M2 by itself. The later return path is outcome-only and cannot be used at the event time. China-specific microstructure, retail participation, T+1 and news/attention alternatives constrain transfer.

## Reconciliation and permitted use

CL-M2-004 is **confirmed but qualified**: liquidity measures are candidate C/R context, and competing shortage/oversupply explanations must stay separate. G2 use: PIT liquidity-state/rival constructs and later-only validation design. Prohibited: equating illiquidity with M2, volume with M2, or importing an intraday reversal rule.
