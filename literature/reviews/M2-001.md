# M2-001 — Coval & Stafford (2007)

**Depth:** SELECTIVE DEEP. **Question:** what can fund-flow-linked trading establish about temporary pressure?

## Full-text admission

*Asset Fire Sales (and Purchases) in Equity Markets*, JFE 86, 479–512. Lawful institutional manuscript: https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/conference/2005/liquidity/Coval_Stafford.pdf. Reviewed 2026-09-04; researcher-authored notes only.

## Decision-relevant extraction

The proposed pressure source is large mutual-fund inflows/outflows causing funds to expand/reduce overlapping holdings. Fund flows and lagged portfolio holdings create a plausible institutional-pressure setting, but are not a clean event-level exogenous shock: flows can be information related and holdings/reporting are delayed. The study associates distress-fund overlap with abnormal price/volume behavior and later reversal, but later reversal is validation, never a contemporaneous M2 label.

PIT candidates: released/known fund-flow context where timing permits, lagged disclosed holdings/overlap, contemporaneous price, volume and liquidity/market context. These can increase M2 belief only with rival-information checks. They do not establish forced motive, information orthogonality, or a portable A-share event label.

## Reconciliation and permitted use

CL-M2-001 is **confirmed but narrowed**: it supports a pressure-source/overlap candidate and the need for PIT disclosure-lag controls; it does not validate a generic fund-flow pressure factor. G2 candidate use: C/R source/overlap lineage and timing/rejection checks. Prohibited: a flow proxy as exogenous M2, later reversal as input, or inherited fund thresholds/horizon.

## Logic, limits and testability

`fund redemption/inflow → trading of overlapping holdings → price/volume response → candidate temporary pressure → later path for validation`. Missing links are trader motive and information orthogonality. Rivals: informed liquidation, common fundamentals/news, risk, liquidity and holdings staleness. Requires later G3 PIT holdings, flow-release and market data; no provider, formula or test is selected.
