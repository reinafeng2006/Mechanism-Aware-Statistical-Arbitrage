## Reading rules

- **MINIMUM REQUIREMENT** is the least conceptual information needed to assess whether a candidate can be constructed as defined.
- **PREFERRED / ENHANCEMENT REQUIREMENT** may improve context, precision or identification but its absence cannot make the simpler candidate infeasible.
- Timing is an envelope (`ordered observations`, `event-time-capable`, `relationship-history-capable`, `finer timing may help`), never an exact interval.
- Historical depth means enough strictly trailing PIT history to estimate/monitor the construct across relevant states; no numerical window is selected.
- Universe coverage means evaluation across the intended production A-share machinery universe and historical membership, not convenient surviving pairs; no threshold is selected.
- Feasibility uses G2-05 statuses and is independent of the Production Data Tier.
- `CORE-FEASIBILITY` means the minimum production architecture depends on the information class being feasibly auditable. `OPTIONAL-ENHANCEMENT` cannot block a simpler architecture. `RESEARCH-ONLY` cannot block production.
- All derived candidates inherit the full PIT, vintage, coverage, latency and feasibility burden of their upstream candidates.
- **`G3A evaluates feasibility, not desirability.`** Feasibility outcomes cannot rank alpha, select a winner, change tiers for attractiveness or resolve G2 choices.

## Target-Universe Feasibility Frame

G3A must assess feasibility across the intended production scope: economically coherent A-share machinery securities and their PIT historical membership, including entrants, exits, suspensions, delistings and sparse-history cases where lawful records exist. Convenient pairs, surviving securities or high-data-quality subsets cannot define feasibility for the whole architecture.

No constituent list, numerical coverage threshold, minimum listing age, liquidity screen or survivorship rule is selected. G3A must report coverage distributions and missingness by candidate, time and relevant subgroup so later G2 decisions can distinguish universally feasible core inputs from optional or research-only extensions.

## Proposed G3A boundary

`G3A — PIT Data / Provider Feasibility Audit` is **PROPOSED ONLY / NOT ACTIVE**.

If separately authorized, G3A may inspect provider/source capabilities, lawful availability, PIT/vintage support, historical/universe coverage, attainable frequency/granularity, latency, source stability, lineage support and expected acquisition/maintenance burden against these envelopes.

G3A may not acquire the formal empirical dataset, compute research outcomes, select winning measurements, inspect strategy performance, fit models or weaken frozen constructs.

Intended controlled loop:

`G2-12 requirement envelopes`

`→ G3A feasibility audit`

`→ return to G2 for implementable measurement specification freeze`

`→ separate authorization for G3B formal data acquisition`

G3B is not proposed for activation and remains locked.
