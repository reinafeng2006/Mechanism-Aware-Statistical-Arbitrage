## Admission rule

An empirical claim is inadmissible until its source license, immutable snapshot identifier, point-in-time semantics, extraction method, transformations, environment, artifact hash, and independent review are recorded. Literature claims require lawful full text, claim-level provenance, permitted-use boundaries, and evidence ancestry. Observatory material remains discovery context, never empirical or literature evidence.
