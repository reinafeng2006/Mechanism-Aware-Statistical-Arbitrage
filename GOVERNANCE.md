# Governance Contract

1. The repository remains at G0 until an explicit approved gate record changes its status.
2. G0 freezes thesis and research boundaries, not downstream measurements, statistics, models, factors, formulas, or implementation.
3. No market data, derived outcomes, formal pair selection, model code, backtests, or performance artifacts may be added at G0.
4. The formal pair-formation process must be company-aware, fully data-driven, point-in-time, reproducible, generalizable, and free of subjective/manual pair assignment.
5. Candidate information families and P/S/C/R roles do not confer validity or freeze operational definitions.
6. Every material claim must have a type and evidence ancestry; every material choice requires a decision ID and amendment history.
7. Point-in-time availability and causal ordering are mandatory; later outcomes may not redefine earlier pipeline stages.
8. Description, prediction, intervention, and economic validation remain separate.
9. M0–M3 are provisional hypotheses and must admit ambiguity, counterevidence, and rejection.
10. Observatory imports remain quarantined discovery context and never count as project evidence.
11. Held-out evidence, deterministic artifacts, and predeclared stopping rules must be specified at their later gates before empirical access.
12. A governance breach triggers STOP and independent review.
