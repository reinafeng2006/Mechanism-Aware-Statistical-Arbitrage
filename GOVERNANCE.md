# Governance Contract

1. G0 was frozen and passed on 2026-09-03; amendments require a new explicit decision and must preserve evidence ancestry.
2. G0 freezes thesis and research boundaries, not downstream measurements, statistics, models, factors, formulas, or implementation.
3. G1 is authorized only for literature and mechanism-evidence design until its search plan receives researcher approval.
4. No market data, derived outcomes, formal pair selection, model code, backtests, or performance artifacts may be added during G1 design.
5. The formal pair-formation process must be company-aware, fully data-driven, point-in-time, reproducible, generalizable, and free of subjective/manual pair assignment.
6. Candidate information families and P/S/C/R roles do not confer validity or freeze operational definitions.
7. Every material claim must have a type and claim-level evidence ancestry; every material choice requires a decision ID and amendment history.
8. Point-in-time availability and causal ordering are mandatory; later outcomes may not redefine earlier pipeline stages.
9. Description, prediction, intervention, and economic validation remain separate.
10. M0–M3 are falsifiable working hypotheses and must admit ambiguity, counterevidence, rejection, and no trade.
11. Observatory imports remain quarantined discovery context and never count as project evidence.
12. Held-out evidence, deterministic artifacts, and quantitative stopping rules must be specified at their later gates before empirical access.
13. A governance breach triggers STOP and independent review.
