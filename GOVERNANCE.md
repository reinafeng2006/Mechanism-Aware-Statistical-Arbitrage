# Governance Contract

1. The repository starts and remains at G0 until an explicit approved gate record changes its status.
2. No market data, derived outcomes, factors, model code, backtests, or performance artifacts may be added at G0.
3. Every material claim must have a type and evidence ancestry.
4. Every material research choice must have a decision ID and amendment history.
5. Point-in-time availability, causal ordering, and held-out custody are design requirements, not cleanup tasks.
6. P/S/C/R classify candidate information; they do not confer validity.
7. M0–M3 are provisional hypotheses and must admit ambiguity, counterevidence, and rejection.
8. Observatory imports remain quarantined discovery context.
9. Deterministic builds require immutable inputs, pinned environments, declared seeds where relevant, and artifact hashes.
10. A governance breach triggers STOP and independent review.
