## Answers within authorized scope

| Question | Round-1 evidence boundary |
|---|---|
| Economic/company similarity | Versioned business-description similarity can represent product-market proximity and firm-specific competitor sets. It cannot establish return co-movement, payoff equivalence, a valid statistical pair, or an M1–M3 event mechanism. |
| Statistical relationship measures | Raw normalized-price distance measures historical path proximity; cointegration recurrence measures persistence of a specified long-run statistical link; replicate/residual methods measure conditional multi-asset return replication; FACL measures factor-adjusted residual correlation with multiplicity control. These are different constructs. |
| Raw versus factor-adjusted/residual | Raw distance preserves common exposures in price paths. Replication/FACL residual constructs aim to remove or condition on common components under model assumptions. Neither is selected as the normal relationship; residual fit is not economic similarity. |
| Stability as validity/rejection information | Chinese recurrence evidence and break-detection literature support stability/break information as a candidate rejection/no-valid-pair layer. They do not prove that a surviving relation is economic, causal, or tradable, and they do not identify M0. |
| Generalizable, outcome-independent construction | Formation/trading separation and FDR control support temporal/multiplicity discipline. No reviewed paper establishes a complete outcome-independent, company-aware, stable, generalizable pair-validity rule. |
| G2 unresolved | PIT company-text transfer; pairwise versus multi-asset residual comparator; non-outcome validity and stability criteria; relation-break versus transient-noise discrimination; joint governance of universe, corporate actions, multiplicity, and detection delay. |
