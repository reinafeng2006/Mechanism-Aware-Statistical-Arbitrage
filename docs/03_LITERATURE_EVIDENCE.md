## Pair Formation synthesis

Economic/product-market proximity, raw distance, return correlation, factor-adjusted/residual dependence, cointegration/stochastic spread, stability/break diagnostics, false-discovery control and global matching have literature ancestry as **non-equivalent constructs**. None is a generally selected pair model. Economic proximity does not establish statistical or trading validity and remains secondary under P1 incremental validation. Positive correlation is not required by the current architecture; signed, asymmetric and state-dependent relationships remain G2 candidates.
