## Approved operative architecture

`Company Representation → Pair Relationship Prior → Abnormality Trigger → Sequential Mechanism Tracking + Dynamic Resolution Prediction → Trade / Update / Reject / Abstain`

The combined fourth element means that mechanism belief and resolution belief can be updated jointly over time as new point-in-time information arrives. It does not require a completed instantaneous mechanism classification before resolution prediction begins.

## Why amend

The original sequential wording could be read as a hard real-time gate: classify a mechanism completely, then predict resolution. The reviewed M1 evidence instead supplies evidence ancestry, timing/identification limits, rival explanations and outcome-only variables. It does not justify a mandatory classification gate. The amendment prevents literature attribution chains from being converted into hard trading gates.

`strong literature attribution evidence ≠ mandatory trading gate`

## Hypotheses and epistemic state

M0–M3 remain competing, falsifiable economic hypotheses, not mandatory mutually exclusive instantaneous labels. `U = Unresolved / Abstain` is an epistemic decision state, not a fifth economic mechanism. Ambiguity, rejection and abstention remain valid outcomes.

## Revised P/S/C/R roles

| Role | Active meaning |
|---|---|
| P — Relationship Prior | Evidence that a pair should normally share a relationship. |
| S — Abnormality Trigger | Evidence that the current relative state is unusual. |
| C — Mechanism Discriminator / Updating Evidence | Contemporaneously arriving information that changes mechanism or resolution belief. |
| R — Rejection / Rival Evidence | Evidence for structural/fundamental invalidation, competing explanations or abstention. |

## G1 reinterpretation

Completed literature artifacts remain valid at their recorded evidence maturity. Their logic chains are reinterpreted as evidence ancestry and identification boundaries for possible sequential evidence inputs—not as real-time classification gates. Future response variables remain validation outcomes only. No claim, permitted-use status, review depth, or artifact is rewritten by this amendment.

## Revised G2 responsibility boundary

G2 must later define three distinct measurement families: (1) trigger variables, (2) mechanism-discriminator variables, and (3) sequential-updating variables. It must decide, without using future information, if and how each family enters a timely sequential decision system. It does not select a Bayesian filter, HMM, state-space model, classifier, factor, threshold, or trade rule through this amendment.

Illustrative only: `belief_(t+1) = update(belief_t, new PIT evidence_(t+1))`. This is **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAUTHORIZED**, not a model specification.
