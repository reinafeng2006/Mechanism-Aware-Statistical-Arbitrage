# G3B-01 Provider & Field Contract — Freeze Decision

Decision ID: **G3B-01**  
Date: **2026-09-08**  
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

The [provider/field contract](../stages/G3B/G3B_01_PROVIDER_FIELD_CONTRACT_PROPOSAL.md) and [field necessity register](../../registers/G3B_LOGICAL_FIELD_NECESSITY_REGISTER.md) are frozen as bounded G3B-02 audit inputs. Logical fields are semantic requirements, not selected physical fields/providers.

E02/E03 are authorized for first-round co-acquisition consideration only. E01 is `AUDIT AUTHORIZED / ACQUISITION CONDITIONAL ON PIT QUALIFICATION`. None is authorized for model use.

Venue-authorized exchange data are the leading canonical candidate class for C01/C03/C04/C05 where applicable, not a selected provider. Opaque adjusted prices cannot be the sole canonical raw record. Event/fundamental fields without defensible historical first-public/available time are barred from event-time use.

G3B-02 is authorized pre-acquisition audit-only. Strictly necessary probes must be quarantined `AUDIT-ONLY / NON-EMPIRICAL`. Formal history/universe download and all relationship, signal, target, outcome and PnL computations remain prohibited.
