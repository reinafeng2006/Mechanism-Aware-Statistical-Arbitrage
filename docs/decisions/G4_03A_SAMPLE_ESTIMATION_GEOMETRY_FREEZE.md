# G4-03A Sample & Estimation Geometry Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-10  
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**  
Scope: pre-empirical statistical protocol only; no frozen-data inspection or computation.

## Frozen decisions

- common estimation histories: H63, H126, H252;
- H504 as a long-history extension restricted to representation families with a preregistered structural/theoretical support reason;
- representation-specific minimum-history support contracts fixed before evaluation and counted in the Search Budget;
- separation of market/abnormality evaluation cadence from normal-relationship refresh cadence;
- U1D, U1W, and U1M as preregistered refresh candidates, with U1D the highest-adaptation candidate rather than a default;
- O1, O5, O10, and O20 as outcome-validation horizons;
- `outcome horizon != trading holding period`;
- multidimensional missingness representation;
- structurally triggered, not universally imposed, purge/embargo;
- symbolic representation-aware Search Budget based on explicit authorized tuples;
- prohibition of silent Cartesian-product expansion and performance-driven parameter expansion.

## Preserved unresolved decisions

Numerical tolerances, exact purge/embargo lengths, representation-specific support thresholds, and selections among the frozen candidate menus remain unresolved. H504 cannot be activated for a representation after outcome inspection merely because that representation performs poorly.

## Boundary

This freeze authorizes no data access, statistic, estimator, representation, parameter value, target, trade rule, or empirical comparison. G4-03B must select the temporal validation architecture before development access.
