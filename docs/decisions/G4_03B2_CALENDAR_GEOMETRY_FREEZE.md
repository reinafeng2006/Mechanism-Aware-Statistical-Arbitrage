# G4-03B2 Calendar Geometry Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-10
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Geometry roles

- `CG2 — PRIMARY CALENDAR GEOMETRY`;
- `CG1 — ROLLING FIXED-SPAN ROBUSTNESS COMPETITOR`;
- `CG0 — EXPANDING-HISTORY SENSITIVITY`.

`CG2 = shared evaluation origins + preregistered representation-specific authorized estimation geometry`.

Representation-specific support may use only G4-03A Search Budget tuples and preregistered support contracts. `representation-specific estimation support != outcome-driven tailoring`.

Competing candidates within the same comparison layer share outer evaluation origins and decision periods, subject only to frozen candidate-specific G4-01 eligibility. Unequal support and exclusion reasons remain visible. CG1 and CG0 cannot become alternative tuning environments after CG2 results.

## Frozen calendar regions

- `2013-01-07 through 2014-12-31`: warm-up / formation reserve only;
- `2015-01-01 through 2023-12-31`: nested development plus outer pseudo-OOS;
- `2024-01-01 through 2025-12-31`: final sealed held-out.

`warm-up information may support estimation but does not constitute development or evaluation evidence`.

The held-out region remains inaccessible. This decision authorizes no data or outcome inspection.

## Unresolved fold geometry

Outer-fold count, spacing/duration, inner origins, rolling/expanding behavior within CG2, purge/embargo lengths, outcome censoring, and native/common-support aggregation remain unresolved for G4-03B3 and later Block C governance.
