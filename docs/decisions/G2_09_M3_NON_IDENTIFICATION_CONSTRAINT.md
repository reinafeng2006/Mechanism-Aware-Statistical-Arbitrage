# G2-09 — M3 Non-Identification Constraint Freeze Decision

Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**
Date: 2026-09-07
Boundary: constraint design only. This document creates no M3 score, factor, classifier, proxy, threshold, model, production signal or trade rule.

## Decision objective

Formalize what the frozen G1 evidence does **not** permit the strategy to infer about M3. M3 remains a legitimate economic research hypothesis, but current evidence supplies no positive contemporaneous identification basis.

Frozen boundaries:

`residual / unexplained abnormality ≠ M3`

`not M1 + not M2 + not M0 ≠ M3`

`future normalization / convergence ≠ event-time M3 evidence`

`opposite-direction movement ≠ M3`

`temporary-looking deviation ≠ positive M3 identification`

These boundaries prevent taxonomy completion from manufacturing a mechanism label.

## Two M3 roles

### M3 Production Signal

Status: **BLOCKED — NO POSITIVE IDENTIFICATION BASIS**.

The current evidence base does not permit a real-time M3 probability, score, factor, proxy, classifier, event-time discriminator or production label. Residual abnormality, failure of other mechanisms, apparent temporariness or later normalization cannot be transformed into production M3 use.

This block is substantive, not merely “measurement pending.” G2 must not invent a candidate M3 measurement family to make M0–M3 symmetric.

### M3 Discovery / Research Hypothesis

Status: **PRESERVED — PERMITTED LATER UNDER FROZEN PROTOCOL**.

The project may retain the hypothesis that a temporary idiosyncratic relative dislocation exists that is not adequately explained by currently identified M1/M2 mechanisms or positive M0 rejection evidence. This possibility is not operationally identified and does not authorize live labeling or trading.

The distinction is:

`economic hypothesis preserved ≠ positive identification available ≠ production use authorized`

## Live-case routing rule

At every live decision origin:

`unexplained abnormality → U / unresolved`

not:

`unexplained abnormality → M3`

Positive structural, fundamental or relationship-invalidating evidence may support M0 under G2-08. PIT source/link/under-response evidence may support M1 belief updates, and pressure-source/liquidity/contamination evidence may support M2 belief updates. Absence or weakness of those evidence chains does not generate M3.

U remains the correct epistemic state when the abnormality is unexplained, rival explanations remain plausible, measurements fail, or evidence is insufficient/conflicting. U is not M3 and does not default to M0.

## Prohibited inference rules

The following transformations are prohibited:

1. residual from a pair/factor/normality model → M3;
2. large or statistically unusual abnormality → M3;
3. negative/opposite-direction response → M3;
4. failure to meet M1 or M2 criteria → M3;
5. absence of positive M0 evidence → M3;
6. apparently temporary price path → M3;
7. later convergence, normalization or profitable mean reversion → original event-time M3;
8. an “other” class in a classifier → economic M3 mechanism;
9. taxonomy completeness or class balance → permission to create M3;
10. poor performance of M1/M2 or desire for new factors → permission to create M3.

Descriptive residual/context fields may remain in research artifacts if clearly labeled non-identifying and routed to U in production.

## Future discovery-use pathway

A later research-only program may investigate whether a distinct M3 process appears to exist. For example, later normalization while the pair relationship remains valid and known M0/M1/M2 explanations are weak may define a **discovery-sample or research-target candidate**.

This pathway must preserve:

- `M3 discovery target ≠ M3 event-time signal ≠ M3 production label`;
- outcome variables may define retrospective research targets but cannot become inputs at their original event time;
- target construction, exclusion rules and competing-explanation controls must be explicit and versioned;
- development and held-out samples must be separated before target-informed construct/model work;
- any candidate PIT discriminator discovered using outcomes must undergo fresh prespecification and genuinely held-out validation;
- historical event-time information sets must be reconstructed independently of later outcomes;
- failed or ambiguous discovery results remain valid and do not compel an M3 class.

Later normalization is therefore useful for hypothesis discovery or validation-target construction only. It does not establish that contemporaneous M3 identification was possible.

## Look-ahead and outcome-leakage protection

1. Freeze each event's original decision time and PIT information set before inspecting its later path.
2. Store future normalization, convergence, persistence and PnL only as outcome-side fields for that origin.
3. Do not retroactively reclassify an event as M3 based on those outcomes.
4. Do not train an event-time M3 label from outcome-defined discovery cases and evaluate it on the same or target-contaminated sample.
5. Preserve evidence ancestry for every future PIT feature independently from outcome ancestry.
6. Maintain deterministic target/version records and development/held-out separation.
7. A later sequential decision may use information genuinely available by that later time, but it cannot rewrite the earlier inference record.

## Reopening conditions

M3 production measurement may be reconsidered only through the frozen G1 reopening governance when at least one decision-critical trigger is documented:

1. new decision-relevant literature provides positive contemporaneous identification ancestry that may change the permitted-use boundary;
2. later internal research identifies a reproducible PIT construct plausibly distinguishing a candidate M3 process from M0/M1/M2/U and exposes a decision-critical evidence gap;
3. internal work exposes a material identification problem or new construct not covered by the frozen G1 synthesis, justifying formal literature reopening;
4. a previously inaccessible decision-relevant source becomes lawfully available and may change the boundary.

Reopening is not automatic. It requires a formal decision record, scoped evidence question, current-project evidence admission and preservation of held-out integrity. Predecessor or Observatory material remains discovery context only until independently admitted.

The following are not reopening triggers: M1/M2 underperformance, poor PnL, unexplained residual cases, desire for additional factors, taxonomy completeness or class-balance concerns.

## Frozen classification

### FROZEN CONSTRAINT

- all five non-identification boundaries;
- separate production and discovery roles;
- production event-time discriminator/signal/model blocked absent positive basis;
- unexplained live cases route to U;
- no inference by residual, exclusion, outcome, direction or apparent temporariness;
- outcome/development/held-out and PIT reconstruction protections;
- frozen G1 reopening governance and non-trigger list.

### RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS

M3 remains `PRESERVED` as a falsifiable economic possibility and may be studied later under the frozen discovery protocol.

### BLOCKED PRODUCTION USE

No M3 event-time discriminator, probability, score, factor, proxy, classifier, label, model or trade input is permitted under the current evidence basis.

### EMPIRICAL-DEFER

Discovery-target definition, outcome-side pattern analysis, reproducible PIT-construct discovery, held-out validation, mechanism distinction, A-share transferability and any evidence capable of supporting a future reopening request.

G2-10 remains unauthorized. No data acquisition, implementation or empirical testing is performed.
