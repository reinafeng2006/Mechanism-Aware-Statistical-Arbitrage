## Incorporated consistency clarifications

1. `PRIMARY IMPLEMENTABLE ≠ SELECTED`: PRIMARY denotes a simple feasible candidate baseline or necessary semantic channel for later comparison, never default adoption, winning status or automatic final-strategy inclusion.
2. N0 is the simpler strongly pooled baseline; N1 is the mandatory pair-specific challenger. Both remain unselected and must undergo the frozen fair relationship-level comparison.
3. `distance ≠ pair validity` and `correlation ≠ normal relationship`; both are baseline candidate representations only.
4. MP0 is an oriented excess-move diagnostic baseline, not an M2 label, temporary-pressure identifier or production classifier. `excess move ≠ temporary pressure`.
5. The Pre-G3B handoff covers event-time inputs and separately quarantined future resolution-validation information. Catch-up, reversal/normalization, persistence, relationship continuation/break, horizon and magnitude are outcome-side only and cannot enter earlier mechanism inference.

## Preserved boundaries

- Semantic fit, evidence permission, G3A feasibility and production complexity informed narrowing; prediction, outcomes, returns and PnL did not.
- Approximation availability cannot redefine a frozen construct.
- Enhancement infeasibility cannot invalidate its simpler parent: `MP3 infeasible ≠ M2 infeasible` and analogous dependency logic applies throughout.
- Competing implementable specifications remain open for later frozen development/OOS comparison.
- M3 event-time/production use remains blocked.
- No provider, field, frequency, estimator, factor, model, threshold, window or trade rule is selected.
- G3B acquisition remains separately locked until explicit approval after its design checkpoint.

Researcher approval freezes G2B and authorizes only initialization of G3B acquisition design, not acquisition itself.
