# G4-04A2 Incremental Relationship-Value Metrics Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-11
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Paired estimands

Baseline and challenger loss are paired at the finest valid matched unit before aggregation.

- `DeltaLoss_N = Loss_N1 - Loss_N0`;
- `DeltaLoss_P = Loss_P1 - Loss_P0`;
- negative DeltaLoss means lower challenger OOS relationship loss.

The matched contract fixes pair, direction, origin, response target, candidate-neutral PIT scale, temporal role, common eligibility, relationship representation, and authorized estimation geometry.

N0-to-N1 isolates additional pair-specific heterogeneity/adaptation. P0-to-P1 isolates the separately tracked Economic/Company Relationship layer while preserving complete P0 information.

## Support, aggregation, and governance

- CS2 common support is primary attribution evidence;
- native support separately reports coverage/deployability;
- directional evidence precedes pair aggregation;
- pairs receive equal influence;
- N and P comparisons remain separate multiplicity families;
- SR0 structural admissibility remains distinct from SR1 comparative evidence;
- no unique winner is forced.

`downstream return prediction / PnL != P1 incremental relationship value`.

No numerical threshold, empirical computation, or candidate selection is authorized.
