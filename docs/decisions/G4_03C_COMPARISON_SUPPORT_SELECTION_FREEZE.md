# G4-03C Comparison, Support & Selection Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-10
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Support architecture

`CS2 — DUAL-SUPPORT ARCHITECTURE` is selected.

- Common support is primary for attributing differences to competing specifications.
- Native support is separately mandatory for coverage, deployability, and practical usefulness.
- Common support respects frozen candidate-specific eligibility and temporal rules and never recodes excluded observations.

`better native-support result != specification superiority when support differs materially`.

`better common-support result != sufficient deployability`.

## Selection architecture

`SR0 -> SR1` is selected.

SR0 is non-compensatory: structural or validity failure cannot be offset by downstream performance. SR1 applies ordered hierarchical dominance to admissible candidates: comparable-support incremental quality, temporal robustness, native coverage/deployability, then production/data complexity as a later discriminator or tie-break.

`SR1 is not required to produce a unique winner`.

Allowed outcomes are dominant/advances, non-dominated/both survive, insufficient differentiation, fails admissibility, and no candidate advances. SR2 requires separate ex-ante authorization and cannot serve as a weighted-score escape hatch.

## Repeated temporal evidence

Semiannual inner origins are repeated pseudo-OOS evidence, not independent winner-picking opportunities. Inner aggregation is frozen before development computation.

OF4 aggregation must preserve aggregate magnitude, directional consistency, fold dispersion, severe fold-specific failure, and eligible support. `one exceptional outer fold must not automatically dominate confirmation`.

## Multiplicity and sensitivities

Multiplicity is dependency ordered across relationship, normality, abnormality, mechanism, and resolution layers. Within-family, across-stage, registered-sensitivity, and reopened/exploratory analyses retain separate status. Upstream failure restricts downstream family expansion.

Registered TP/CG/fold/origin sensitivities remain outside primary selection unless separately authorized in the frozen sensitivity budget and cannot rescue an unsuccessful primary specification.

Exact metrics, aggregation statistics, thresholds, multiplicity procedures, and sensitivity budget remain unresolved for G4-04.
