# G4-04A1c Hard Failure & Continuous Evidence Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-11
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Hard scale failures

Scaled relationship loss is unavailable only when the PIT scale is unavailable, non-finite, exactly zero, or in a mathematically unsupported estimator state. Near-zero but nondegenerate scale remains continuous scale-quality evidence; no epsilon replacement or arbitrary hard boundary is permitted.

## Continuous evidence

Near-zero scale quality, estimator/support precision, common-support retention, absolute relationship loss, and comparative deterioration/reversal magnitude and uncertainty remain continuous evidence. Lack of a hard threshold does not force discretization or a winner.

`common-support limitation affects comparative claim scope; it does not by itself imply model inadmissibility`.

CS2 native-support coverage remains separately mandatory.

## Removed thresholds

SQ05/SQ10, MS80/MS90, SF0-2/SF0-3, SF1-10/SF1-20, and SF2-50/SF2-70 are permanently registered `REMOVED AS ARBITRARY`. Reintroduction after outcome inspection requires explicit reopening, independent justification, a new protocol version, and contamination recording.

## OF4 veto and SR1

`two or more independently justified severe OF4 failures are incompatible with ADVANCES / DOMINANT status`.

Zero or one severe failure does not establish advancement. The veto operates only after severe failure itself has an independently justified, preregistered definition.

SR1 may return `DOMINANT / ADVANCES`, `NON-DOMINATED / BOTH SURVIVE`, `INSUFFICIENT DIFFERENTIATION`, or `NO CANDIDATE ADVANCES`. No numerical threshold may be invented merely to force a unique disposition.
