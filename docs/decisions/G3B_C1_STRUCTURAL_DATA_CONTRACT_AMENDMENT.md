# G3B-C1 Structural Data Contract Amendment

Decision: **APPROVED / FROZEN — 2026-09-10**

The researcher approved the two-layer separation documented in [the G3B-C1 amendment](../stages/G3B/G3B_C1_STRUCTURAL_DATA_CONTRACT_AMENDMENT_PROPOSAL.md):

`Core Dataset Structural Freeze ≠ Candidate Measurement Authorization`

The decision freezes the [Core Dataset Freeze Criteria](../stages/G3B/CORE_DATASET_FREEZE_CRITERIA.md), the [C04/C05 Measurement Eligibility Matrix](../stages/G3B/C04_C05_MEASUREMENT_ELIGIBILITY_MATRIX.md), C04 as `AUTHORITATIVE COVERAGE INCOMPLETE`, the four C05 states, and `security identifier continuity ≠ economic relationship continuity`.

It additionally freezes mandatory downstream propagation: unresolved C04/C05 states cannot be cleared, reset, imputed, or silently recoded by dataset freeze or transformation. Candidate-specific eligibility must be enforced before computation and remain auditable by rule/version lineage.

The decision does not freeze the dataset, modify G2B classifications, authorize a measurement, permit adjusted-price construction, or activate G4.
