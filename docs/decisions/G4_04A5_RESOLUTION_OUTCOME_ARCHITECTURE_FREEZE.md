# G4-04A5 Resolution / Outcome Architecture Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-11
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Frozen architecture

- resolution is evaluated as future evolution relative to the frozen event-time abnormality, relationship state, expected response, source/peer roles, and information set;
- the primary outcome state is a four-component, mechanism-neutral continuous vector containing peer/follower catch-up, source normalization, persistence/non-resolution, and relationship continuation/change-break;
- M1 peer/follower catch-up is distinct from M2 source/shocked-asset normalization;
- persistence/non-resolution and relationship continuation/change-break remain independent outcome channels;
- outcome components may coexist and are not forced into mutually exclusive M1/M2 labels;
- no universal convergence score is authorized;
- O1, O5, O10, and O20 remain the preregistered outcome-validation horizons;
- target-horizon-aware censoring applies, and no 2023 outer-fold outcome may cross into the sealed 2024–2025 held-out region;
- `future information evaluates the event; it does not rewrite the event`;
- `resolution target != trading profitability`;
- `outcome horizon != holding period`.

Exact formulas, categorical discretization, numerical thresholds, and aggregation rules remain unresolved. No target computation, outcome inspection, trading rule, or held-out access is authorized by this freeze.
