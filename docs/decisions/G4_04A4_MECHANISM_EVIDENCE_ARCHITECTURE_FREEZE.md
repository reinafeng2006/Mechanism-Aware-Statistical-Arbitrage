# G4-04A4 Mechanism-Evidence Architecture Freeze

Decision date: 2026-09-11
Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN**

## Evidence architecture

- mechanism-evidence records are append-only, versioned, PIT-timestamped, provenance-preserving, and role-specific;
- `measurement != evidence != belief != probability != label != decision`;
- M1 under-response morphology is separate from contextual M1 evidence and rivals;
- M2 excess movement is separate from temporary-pressure evidence;
- every M2 proxy carries mandatory contamination metadata;
- M0 is asymmetric positive rejection evidence; `absence of M1/M2 evidence != M0`;
- U is an overlapping dynamic epistemic state, not an economic mechanism or residual score;
- G2-09 M3 production prohibitions remain exact;
- evidence may simultaneously support or oppose multiple mechanisms;
- evidence quantities need not sum to one;
- sequential evidence-state history is immutable and superseded only through versioned append/amendment lineage;
- `repeated computation of unchanged information != new evidence`;
- future catch-up, reversal, normalization, persistence, and PnL remain outcome-only and prohibited from originating event-time evidence.

All formulas, proxies, factors, weights, probabilities, classifiers, and belief-update models remain unresolved. No data access or computation is authorized.
