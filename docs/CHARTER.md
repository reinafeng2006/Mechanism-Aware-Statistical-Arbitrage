# Research Charter

## Research question

Can ex-ante, point-in-time information distinguish mechanisms behind abnormal relative-price states in economically related A-share machinery-stock pairs well enough to improve resolution prediction and a subsequent trade/no-trade policy after realistic frictions?

## Unit of inquiry

A timestamped pair-event: a pre-eligible security pair, an abnormal relative state detected at a decision time, the information set legally and operationally available then, a provisional mechanism hypothesis, a future resolution outcome, and a later economic decision. Exact definitions remain G0 decisions.

## Claims are separated

1. **Description:** characterize P/S/C/R variables and observed dislocations.
2. **Prediction:** forecast resolution using information available at the decision time.
3. **Intervention:** compare an explicit trade/no-trade policy with a predeclared comparator.
4. **Economic validation:** test net implementability under point-in-time eligibility, costs, constraints, capacity, and held-out evaluation.

Evidence for one layer does not establish the next.

## Scope lock

This initialization freezes the conceptual pipeline and mechanism hypotheses only. It does not freeze construct operationalizations, labels, horizons, universe, benchmarks, or algorithms. Those require gated decisions.

## Governance principles

- **Freeze before inspection:** definitions, exclusions, splits, metrics, and stopping rules precede outcome access.
- **Causal timing:** every field has event time, availability time, ingestion time, and decision-use time.
- **Point-in-time information:** later revisions, classifications, corporate actions, and survivorship knowledge are barred unless reconstructed as then-known.
- **Explicit decision records:** material choices receive an ID, owner, date, rationale, alternatives, dependencies, and amendment history.
- **Development/held-out separation:** held-out outcomes are inaccessible until a documented gate authorizes one bounded use.
- **Evidence ancestry:** claims trace to immutable source snapshots, transformations, parameters, code/environment identity, and reviewers.
- **Deterministic artifacts:** identical declared inputs and environment must reproduce the same artifact and hash.
- **Stopping rules:** futility, instability, leakage, identification failure, and governance breaches can stop the project.
- **No retrospective relabeling:** mechanism definitions cannot be repaired using the outcomes they are meant to predict.

## Roles

Research lead owns the thesis and approvals; data steward owns point-in-time provenance; methods lead owns identification and validation design; independent reviewer controls held-out access and gate audits. Named people remain unresolved at G0.
