# G2 Measurement Design Synthesis / Pre-G3 Readiness Checkpoint

Status: **CONCLUSION APPROVED — PRE-DATA BLOCKERS CONSOLIDATED INTO G2-12**
Date: 2026-09-07
Authority: G2-01 through G2-11 approved/frozen.
Boundary: readiness audit only. G3 remains locked. This checkpoint selects no measurement, clock, frequency, provider, database, implementation or empirical winner.

## Executive readiness conclusion

**NOT READY FOR DATA ACQUISITION; CONDITIONALLY READY FOR A SEPARATELY AUTHORIZED G3 DATA-FEASIBILITY AUDIT AFTER A SMALL PRE-G3 CONTRACT IS RESOLVED.**

The semantic architecture is coherent and prevents forced mechanism labels, outcome leakage and timestamp ambiguity. It is not yet a measurement specification. The genuine pre-data blocker is the absence of a bounded, versioned **Candidate Measurement Requirement Envelope** for each retained architecture family, together with target-universe and decision-use frames sufficient to make coverage and latency feasibility meaningful. Without them, a provider audit would be unbounded and could let data convenience define the construct.

This does not require selecting winners, formulas or providers. It requires declaring which candidate families G3 must assess, their minimum conceptual inputs, permissible substitutes, timing/coverage need categories, dependencies and production/research-only tiers.

## 3. Unresolved items by responsible decision time

### RESOLVE BEFORE G3

1. **Candidate Measurement Requirement Envelope** — retained candidate families, minimum conceptual input fields, acceptable source/substitute classes, PIT metadata, frequency/latency categories, history/coverage needs and production/research-only status. This is the genuine pre-data blocker.
2. **Target-universe feasibility frame** — intended A-share machinery universe semantics and minimum coverage questions, without acquiring constituents.
3. **Decision-use clock envelope** — broad regimes G3 must audit, without selecting an interval.
4. **Candidate dependency graph** — inherited Expected Response, uncertainty, relationship, event and flow requirements; no double counting/conditioning.
5. **Minimum versus optional data tiers** — production-core, hard/optional and research-only audit scope.

### G3 DATA-FEASIBILITY INFORMED

- feasible source classes and historical vintages for market, disclosure, event, linkage, exposure, liquidity/flow and trade/quote data;
- timestamp semantics/precision, calendar and publication/access latency;
- attainable granularity, history, universe, sparse-history, delisting and survivorship coverage;
- revision/restatement reconstruction;
- feasibility of P1, MP2/MP3, R1/R2 and dedicated U diagnostics;
- physical lineage, reproducible identifiers, cost and maintenance burden.

G3 findings may constrain candidates but cannot redefine constructs or select winners by convenience.

### RESOLVE AFTER G3 / BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION

- retained P0 representations and P1 test specification;
- N0/N1 estimators and any N2 escalation request;
- Expected Response and uncertainty estimators;
- abnormality measurements, normalization and summary;
- actual clocks, windows, update cadence and latency tolerance;
- operational UR, MP and R subset/specifications;
- U and belief/update representation;
- missing/stale/conflict treatment;
- deterministic transformations, physical schemas, acceptance tests and relationship-level validation protocol.

### EMPIRICAL VALIDATION

- P0/P1 incremental relationship value;
- N0/N1 and possible N2 benefit;
- relationship representation, stability, calibration, generalization, false-pair and break behavior;
- abnormality calibration/false triggers;
- M1/M2 discrimination, contamination and resolution prediction;
- M0 false rejection and U false resolution/coverage;
- frequency/latency incremental value, production burden, A-share transferability, held-out and after-cost economics.

## 5. Circular dependencies and controlled break

1. Measurement choice needs feasibility facts; G3 needs a bounded candidate envelope.
2. Desired decision clock determines requirements; actual source latency constrains plausible clocks.
3. Model/uncertainty choices determine lineage/history; available PIT history constrains estimability.

Approved checkpoint direction, implemented as the G2-12 proposal:

`G2 candidate envelopes + broad clock/use regimes`

`→ G3 feasibility audit only, without acquisition or winner selection`

`→ G2 candidate narrowing and measurement-specification freeze`

`→ separate acquisition authorization`

## 7. Checkpoint answers

1. Frozen semantics/constraints are complete across relationship, abnormality, roles, M0/M1/M2/M3/U, timing, lineage, leakage and feasibility governance.
2. Competing specifications remain deliberately open as listed above.
3. Items 1–5 in the queue must be resolved before G3 or acquisition.
4. Provider/source/vintage/coverage/latency/reconstruction feasibility requires G3.
5. Comparative validity, identification, prediction and economics remain empirical.
6. Candidate data requirements are not yet one-to-one sufficient; a bounded envelope is missing.
7. Circularities exist but a two-pass G2 envelope → G3 feasibility → G2 specification process can break them.
8. G2 has a genuine narrow pre-data blocker. It is not ready for acquisition or immediate G3 activation; after items 1–5, it may be ready for a separately approved feasibility-only handoff.
