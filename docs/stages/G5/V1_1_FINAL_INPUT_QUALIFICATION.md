The official calendar extension passes the original bounded C04-A contract.
This does **not** retroactively qualify an immutable model input that omitted
newly identified action-date exclusions.

Before any PnL computation/inspection, an eligibility-only comparison found:

| Immutable input | New action dates still marked response-eligible | Scope | Trading disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Inner through 2019 | 0 | Original qualified C04 ancestry | Execute frozen independent inner folds |
| OF4 through 2023 | 1,597 | 554 securities, 2020–2023 | Complete economic books unavailable |
| Final held-out input through 2025 | 2,825 | 623 securities, 2020–2025 including formation history | Complete economic books unavailable |

The final input total includes 610 discrepancies in 2024 and 618 in 2025;
the other 1,597 are its historical formation ancestry. Exact security/date
scope is recorded in the [qualification manifest](../../../data/manifests/TRADING_V1_1_INPUT_QUALIFICATION.json).
This checks Boolean eligibility and official dates, not returns or performance.

The frozen prediction ancestors cannot be refitted or silently repaired during
this final acquisition/closure action. A new trading overlay cannot make those
original estimation inputs obey an exclusion they did not implement. Later
complete books therefore fail the full input-lineage prerequisite, without
selecting favourable episodes or substituting a candidate. No later PnL is
calculated. This is an input-qualification limitation, not negative trading
performance and not a claim that every individual predicted observation fails.

Published OF4/held-out research vectors remain immutable historical descriptive
outputs under the then-used, through-2019 C04 calendar. They must not be promoted
to validated confirmation under the new through-2025 exclusion contract. The
final synthesis must disclose this limitation alongside the existing H4 cutoff.

For qualified inner folds only, the one-shot runner retains exact immutable A3
anchors, both channels, candidate-neutral MP1, FD-A/EP-A/DECA-A, Option-A
simultaneous N=A+B allocation, and all three costs. T09 terminates complete-book
evaluability at the first unqualified held interval; it does not sell/drop that
episode, restart capital, or renormalize survivors. Private qualified-prefix
paths and preserved episode states remain auditable. Full-fold metrics are
unavailable in such a case; subsequent NAV-dependent funding coverage is not
invented. All six candidates and every unavailable scope remain in the ledger.

No policy, candidate, threshold, C04 taxonomy, estimator or frozen payload was
changed to address this finding. No new data-repair branch or V2 is initiated.
