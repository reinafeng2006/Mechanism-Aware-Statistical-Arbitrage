# G4-03B3 Nested Fold Count, Origin Structure & Boundary Geometry Proposal

Status: **PROPOSED / AWAITING RESEARCHER REVIEW**
Boundary: calendar/protocol design only. No frozen-data statistics, availability counts, returns, pairs, measurements, targets, or outcomes may be inspected.

## Objective

Choose a finite nested-fold geometry inside the frozen 2015-01-01 through 2023-12-31 development region while keeping 2013-01-07 through 2014-12-31 as warm-up only and 2024-01-01 through 2025-12-31 sealed.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- outer origins and decision periods are shared within each comparison layer;
- H63/H126/H252 and representation-restricted H504 are the only history menu;
- O20 is the maximum registered outcome horizon;
- inner development, outer pseudo-OOS, and final held-out remain distinct;
- fold boundaries are calendar/protocol determined, never moved for support counts or results;
- outer aggregation/selection rules are frozen before outer inspection;
- boundary targets are censored or excluded under a preregistered purge/embargo rule;
- unequal candidate eligibility is exposed rather than repaired through origin changes.

## Bounded outer-fold candidates

### OF3 — Three broad outer folds

Three chronologically ordered pseudo-OOS blocks inside 2015–2023. Fewer folds reduce repeated evaluation and multiplicity but provide coarser evidence about temporal stability.

### OF4 — Four intermediate outer folds

Four ordered blocks balance repeated temporal assessment against adequate block duration and is the proposed central candidate. Exact calendar boundaries remain unresolved.

### OF6 — Six shorter outer folds

Six ordered blocks increase stability observations and approximate more frequent deployment cycles, but create stronger overlap, multiplicity, and dependence-control burdens.

No other fold count is authorized by this proposal. These labels are protocol candidates, not selected values.

## Inner-origin candidates

- **IO-A — fixed sparse origins:** a small fixed number of time-ordered inner origins per outer training region;
- **IO-B — cadence-bound origins:** origins generated by one frozen U1D/U1W/U1M relationship-refresh candidate, then thinned by an explicit protocol rule to prevent uncontrolled search expansion;
- **IO-C — boundary-anchored origins:** origins placed at prespecified calendar boundaries within the inner region.

Inner origins provide selection evidence only. They do not move outer boundaries or consume the final held-out region.

## Boundary geometry contract to resolve

For each outer fold, bind:

`eligible warm-up/history cutoff -> inner origins -> inner selection cutoff -> purge/embargo -> outer start -> outer end/outcome censoring -> next fold`

The protocol must decide whether CG2 histories roll or expand for each preregistered representation tuple. This decision may vary by authorized representation contract but cannot be outcome-driven.

## Proposed comparison criteria

Compare OF3/OF4/OF6 and IO-A/IO-B/IO-C using protocol geometry only:

- sufficient chronological separation for TP2 roles;
- compatibility with maximum H504 and O20 geometry;
- number of outer evidence consumptions;
- dependence and multiplicity burden;
- live-deployment realism;
- representation-aware Search Budget size;
- transparent native/common-support reporting;
- feasibility of a meaningful final inner selection cutoff before each outer block.

## Unresolved researcher decisions

1. select OF3, OF4, or OF6;
2. select IO-A, IO-B, or IO-C;
3. select exact calendar boundaries and outer durations;
4. select rolling/expanding behavior for each authorized representation geometry;
5. freeze purge/embargo derivation and exact lengths;
6. freeze O1/O5/O10/O20 boundary censoring;
7. freeze outer-fold aggregation and selection/confirmation rule;
8. bind versions, hashes, access logs, and contamination consequences.

No option is selected and no development access is authorized here.
