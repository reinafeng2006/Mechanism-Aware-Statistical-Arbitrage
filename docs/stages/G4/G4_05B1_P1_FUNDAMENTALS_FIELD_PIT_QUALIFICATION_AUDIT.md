## Qualification rule

`QUALIFIED` requires documented field semantics plus defensible historical first-public/available time, preserved vintage/restatement lineage, 2013–2025 A-share coverage capability, deterministic identifier linkage, and reproducibility. A field existing in today's API schema is not enough. `QUALIFIED WITH LIMITATIONS` is used only where the logical capability exists and the remaining limitation is explicitly carryable; `UNRESOLVED` means a hard PIT or lineage question cannot be established from the current non-authenticated contract; `NOT QUALIFIED` means documented behavior conflicts with frozen use.

Official Tushare documentation identifies income-statement `ann_date`, `f_ann_date`, `end_date`, `report_type`, `revenue`, `operate_profit`, and `n_income`; balance-sheet `total_assets`, `total_liab`, and `total_hldr_eqy_exc_min_int`; financial-indicator `ann_date`, `end_date`, `update_flag`, and `roa`; daily-basic historical market-cap/share fields; and security-master listing fields. These pages document present schema capability, not a complete as-of-vintage service contract: [income statement](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=33), [balance sheet](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=36), [financial indicators](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=79), [cash flow](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=44), [daily basic](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=32), [stock basic](https://tushare.pro/document/1?doc_id=25), and [disclosure schedule](https://tushare.pro/document/2?doc_id=162).

## Cross-cutting findings

- Public report schemas make a later authenticated, non-empirical schema/vintage test feasible; they do not prove that historical queries reconstruct exactly what was available at each decision time.
- `ann_date`/`f_ann_date` must be verified against first public dissemination; retrieval time and fiscal period end cannot substitute for `available_time`.
- `update_flag` and report types are useful version indicators but do not establish immutable historical vintages or an as-of-query guarantee by themselves.
- Frozen C01 stable identity and code-change lineage must be the join authority. Current `stock_basic` is a capability input, not a versioned historical identifier master.
- Robustness variables remain inactive. No PIT-unqualified primary is replaced with a convenient provider ratio, and primary and robustness fields are not admitted together.

## Readiness decision

No five-construct P1 enhancement layer is acquisition-ready from public documentation alone. The scale primary has a usable dated-field path with documented limitations; every accounting-derived construct remains `UNRESOLVED` until the researcher authorizes an authenticated schema/vintage qualification or supplies an authoritative disclosure-vintage contract. No acquisition or provider expansion follows from this finding.
