# G4-04A Stage-Specific Metric & Decision-Threshold Specification Proposal

Status: **PROPOSED / AWAITING RESEARCHER REVIEW**
Boundary: pre-empirical specification only. No frozen-data inspection, descriptive statistic, candidate computation, target construction, or outcome access.

## Objective

Translate the frozen G4-04 governance architecture into finite stage-specific metric and decision-threshold menus before development computation. The task must specify what constitutes admissibility, meaningful improvement, uncertainty, temporal robustness, non-dominance, insufficient differentiation, severe failure, and no advancement without using observed results.

## Block A — Relationship representation and validity

Specify bounded metrics for:

- OOS expected-response error or proper loss;
- calibration/uncertainty coverage where the candidate supports uncertainty;
- relationship stability and break behavior;
- cross-pair or cross-time generalization;
- common-support attribution and native-support coverage.

The block must separately define SR0 validity/admissibility requirements and SR1 meaningful incremental quality for competing relationship representations. Trading outcomes are excluded.

## Block B — Normal-relationship specifications

Specify fair-comparison metrics and thresholds for N0 versus N1 and P0 versus P1 under identical semantic target, PIT boundary, temporal geometry, and CS2 support rules. Required dimensions include relationship error/calibration, stability, sparse-history behavior, uncertainty, and generalization. N2 retains its frozen escalation condition and is not automatically admitted.

## Block C — Abnormality measurement

Specify metrics for morphology preservation, unusual-response ranking/calibration, false-abnormality behavior, uncertainty sensitivity, and separation of temporary abnormality from relationship-change evidence. No metric may convert abnormality into a mechanism label or trading opportunity.

## Block D — M1/M2/M0/U evidence

Specify role-appropriate metrics for permitted event-time evidence and rivals:

- M1 response-gap/under-response candidates without equating a gap with identification;
- M2 excess-move, pressure-source, liquidity/flow, contamination, and rival components without equating excess move with pressure;
- M0 positive rejection evidence and U uncertainty/ambiguity representations;
- M3 remains blocked from production evaluation.

Mechanism metrics must preserve PIT availability and cannot use future catch-up, reversal, or normalization as event-time inputs.

## Block E — Resolution/predictive validation

Specify proper metrics for future direction, magnitude, timing, persistence/non-resolution, calibration, and support under quarantined O1/O5/O10/O20 outcomes. Outcome metrics cannot retroactively identify an event-time mechanism. Economic metrics remain deferred.

## Cross-block threshold contract

For every proposed metric, record:

- stage and estimand;
- mathematical definition and direction;
- common-support and native-support role;
- aggregation level: record, inner origin, outer fold, or across folds;
- minimum meaningful effect rationale;
- uncertainty representation;
- admissibility versus performance status;
- severe-failure interpretation;
- multiplicity family and claim role;
- missing/undefined handling;
- frozen decision disposition produced by the rule;
- version and Search Budget entry.

No universal improvement percentage, significance threshold, alpha/q level, or aggregate statistic is proposed here.

## Dependency-ordered decisions

1. choose the bounded primary and supporting metric set for relationship representation/validity;
2. choose N0/N1 and P0/P1 relationship-level estimands;
3. choose abnormality-quality metric families;
4. choose permitted M1/M2/M0/U evidence metric families;
5. choose resolution metric families while retaining outcome quarantine;
6. define which quantities are SR0 admissibility rules versus SR1 performance rules;
7. define stage-specific meaningful-effect and uncertainty candidate rules;
8. define numerical severe-failure, non-dominance, insufficient-differentiation, and no-advance candidates;
9. hand the chosen finite families to later aggregation and multiplicity specification before computation.

No metric, formula, threshold, or candidate winner is selected by initialization.
