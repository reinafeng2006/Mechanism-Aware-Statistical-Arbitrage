## Minimum viable architecture

The minimum bundle preserves raw observations, identity/universe PIT lineage, explicit missingness, immutable source snapshots and outcome quarantine. It does not require every enhancement or competing specification to be immediately implementable.

1. C01 uses reproducibly assembled official SSE/SZSE listing/security records, joined to frozen C06 snapshots through stable internal IDs. Unresolved historical aliases remain explicit.
2. C03 must use raw/unadjusted daily observations. No currently executable source is both higher-qualified and available without new paid/institutional access. Sina is executable but remains `FALLBACK ONLY`; formal use requires the explicit decision in the readiness record.
3. C04 retains original official/company disclosures as authoritative action ancestry. Raw acquisition may proceed while adjusted representations remain constrained.
4. C05 uses available official stop/resume/trading records with limitations. An unexplained missing market row remains `UNKNOWN MISSINGNESS`.
5. E02 is split into directly observed activity (A) and denominator-dependent measures (B).
6. E03 is split into benchmark price history (A) and historical constituents/weights/exposure lineage (B).

`QUALIFIED WITH LIMITATIONS ≠ automatically blocked`  
`operationally useful ≠ canonical-qualified`  
`missing market observation ≠ suspension`
