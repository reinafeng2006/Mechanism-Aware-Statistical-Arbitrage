# G1 Stage Hierarchy

Status: **APPROVED GOVERNANCE — 2026-09-03**

G1 is the single literature and mechanism-evidence gate. The literature-review workflow is nested inside G1 and is not a separate or competing project pipeline.

## Stage responsibility boundary

- **G1 — what existing evidence says deserves measurement and why.** G1 admits and synthesizes literature claims, defines permitted downstream construct use, and records unresolved gaps. It does not operationalize measures.
- **G2 — how those constructs will be operationally measured.** G2 defines constructs, observables, clocks, transformations, validity tests, and rejection logic. It does not select providers or acquire/build the database.
- **G3 — what point-in-time data/database is required and how it will be acquired.** G3 defines fields, vintages, source classes/providers, licensing, acquisition, storage, lineage, and database QA under the frozen G2 measurement specification. It does not inspect research outcomes.

## Nested adaptive workflow

`Discovery → Triage → Light → Selective Deep → Synthesis Checkpoint`

- Discovery and Triage belong to G1-01.
- Light and Selective Deep belong to G1-02.
- Selective Deep is triggered only when decision-relevant; it is never mandatory for every candidate.
- Adaptive stopping applies when further reading is unlikely to change permitted-use boundaries, missing-link assessments, or downstream measurement choices.
- The Synthesis Checkpoint belongs to G1-03. G1-02 may create provisional comparison tables, but they are inputs—not formal cross-paper synthesis or frozen downstream authorization.
- Only G1-04 may freeze evidence and authorize a G2 handoff.
