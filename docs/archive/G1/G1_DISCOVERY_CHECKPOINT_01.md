## Major evidence gaps

1. Pair quality is commonly judged through later trading returns, leaving limited outcome-independent validation.
2. Point-in-time reconstruction of company text, links, classifications, factor exposures, and relationship stability is under-discussed.
3. China/A-share evidence exists for information diffusion and reversal, but machinery-sector transfer is unestablished.
4. Raw versus factor-adjusted relationship quality lacks a decisive comparative literature.
5. M3 is especially vulnerable to circular residual labeling.
6. Flow-pressure proxies can mechanically contain realized returns; Wardlaw (2020) makes this a mandatory validity check.
7. Reversal is non-identifying: liquidity shortage, liquidity oversupply, overreaction/salience, and nonfundamental residual shocks can produce similar paths.
8. Direct contemporaneous M1/M2/M3/M0 discrimination with uncertainty or abstention is sparse.
9. Mechanism-conditioned OOS resolution and net trade/no-trade value are rarely evaluated as separate layers.
10. Full text remains pending for several China-specific high-value papers.
