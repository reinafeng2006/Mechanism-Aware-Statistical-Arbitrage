# G2 Handoff Contract

Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN — 2026-09-04**

G2 is authorized to design measurements for a sequential belief-updating system, not a mandatory M0–M3 classifier. Abnormality, mechanism evidence, and resolution evidence may remain continuous and uncertain. Sequential updating, ambiguity, rejection, and abstention must be preserved; discrete state assignment is not required.

## 1. Constructs G2 would be authorized to operationalize

Competing market-relationship representations and stability/break diagnostics (CL-PF-002–007/009); secondary economic proximity (CL-PF-001); FDR/overlap governance (CL-PF-006/010); continuous pair-specific abnormality; M1 source/link/timing/rival components (CL-M1-001/003); M2 pressure/liquidity/contamination/rival components (CL-M2-001–004, CL-B4-002); positive M0 rejection evidence (CL-M0-001, CL-PF-001/004/005); and U abstention.

## 2. Competing specifications to preserve

Raw versus residual and other non-equivalent representations; P0 versus P1; N0 versus N1; N2 only as later justified complexity; positive/negative/asymmetric/state-dependent relationships; continuous score versus fixed/soft/probabilistic trigger; and competing sequential-update structures. None is selected.

## 3. Questions G2 must resolve

Construct semantics and PIT timing; fair P0/P1 and N0/N1 comparisons; signed conditional response; abnormality dimensions/uncertainty; trigger/discriminator/updating separation; M1 under-response; M2 proxy contamination; break versus temporary state; U/rejection behavior; latency/frequency; and relationship-level validation before PnL.

## 4. Literature-derived requirements passed toward G3

Versioned issuer text; PIT prices/returns and corporate actions; trailing factor/residual/dependence history; relationship persistence/break diagnostics; directed-link vintages; first-public news/events; audited flows and lagged holdings; official calendar/settlement context; trades/quotes/depth/turnover; fundamental announcements/forecast revisions; structural/link changes; and quarantined outcome paths. See `G1_03_DATA_REQUIREMENT_MAP.md`. DR-008 is excluded because its source is not admitted.

## 5. Prohibited shortcuts

No outcome leakage; no hard M1/M2 gate; no positive M3 label; no M0 by elimination; no U as mechanism; no company proximity as core without P1 incremental validation; no PnL-first pair validation; no generic flow/volume/illiquidity/lead-lag label; no post-hoc N1/N2; no selected formula/model/threshold/provider by literature alone.

## 6. Authorization ancestry

Pair relationship: CL-PF-001–007/009/010. M1: CL-M1-001/003. M2: CL-M2-001–004 and CL-B4-002. M3 boundary: CL-M3-001, CL-PF-007, CL-B4-001. M0/U: CL-M0-001 plus the competing-mechanism claims above. Every authorization must retain its claim/review path.

## 7. Limitations G2 must not pretend are solved

No universal relationship representation; no established P0/P1 or N0/N1 winner; no positive M3 identification; no reliable contemporaneous four-way classifier; no proven A-share machinery transfer; incomplete public-versus-private information timing; delayed holdings/fundamental sources; model-conditional microstructure decomposition; and unresolved optimal data frequency.

This contract became active through explicit researcher approval of G1-04 on 2026-09-04. It authorizes G2 initialization and measurement/timing design only. It does not authorize G3, data acquisition, implementation or empirical inspection.
