## Stopping judgment

Within the authorized scope, further routine review was unlikely to change the current permitted-use boundaries:

- Outcome-independent statistical control is supported, but no admitted paper establishes that a pair is economically valid, stable, and generalizable independently of a downstream return outcome.
- Raw and residual representations are literature-supported alternative objects. No admitted direct, full-text, outcome-independent comparison selects one as the universally defensible normal pair relationship.
- Microstructure models can identify a narrow permanent-versus-transitory price-pressure decomposition when their required high-frequency inputs and structural assumptions hold. They do not positively identify M3, or jointly separate M1/M2/M3/M0 at the event time.

This is a bounded evidence result, not an assertion that no such literature exists. It leaves the stated gaps open for G1-03 synthesis and, if authorized, later G2 validation design. The absence of a positive M3 identity is retained as substantive evidence rather than a reason to broaden discovery.
