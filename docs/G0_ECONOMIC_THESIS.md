# G0 — Economic Thesis

Status: **STOP. NOT APPROVED.** No empirical work is authorized.

## Frozen thesis skeleton

For ex-ante eligible pairs of economically related A-share machinery stocks, an abnormal relative state may arise through M1, M2, M3, or M0. Information classified by P/S/C/R and available at the decision time may help identify or reject mechanisms, predict how the state resolves, and support a trade/no-trade decision. The project must separately validate description, prediction, intervention, and net economic value.

## Falsifiable directional hypotheses

- **H1/M1:** after a qualifying peer-relevant information event, the lagging/follower leg contributes more to relative convergence than the leader, within a predeclared horizon.
- **H2/M2:** after qualifying temporary liquidity/flow pressure, the directly shocked leg reverses more than its peer moves, conditional on no R evidence.
- **H3/M3:** after a qualifying idiosyncratic relative displacement without identified diffusion or flow pressure, relative prices normalize within a predeclared horizon.
- **H0/M0:** when structural, fundamental, or unresolved divergence evidence is present—or identification confidence is inadequate—trading does not improve the predeclared net objective versus no trade/comparator.

These are hypotheses only. “Convergence,” “shock,” “follower,” “normalization,” mechanism membership, confidence, and horizons are deliberately undefined pending approval.
