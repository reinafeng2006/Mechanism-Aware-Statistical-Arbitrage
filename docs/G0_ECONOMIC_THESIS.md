# G0 — Economic Thesis

Status: **STOP. NOT APPROVED.** No empirical work is authorized.

## What G0 does—and does not—freeze

G0 freezes the economic thesis and research boundaries. It does not choose constructs, data fields, factors, formulas, algorithms, thresholds, labels, horizons, sample splits, estimators, costs, or trading implementation. Those decisions require literature, measurement, statistical, or implementation work at later gates and must not be answered now by assumption.

## Frozen conceptual pipeline

`Company Representation → Data-Driven Pair Formation → Normal Pair Relationship → Abnormal Relative State → Mechanism Identification → Resolution Prediction → Trade / No-Trade Decision`

## Frozen thesis skeleton

For companies within the conceptual scope of A-share machinery, a company-aware and reproducible point-in-time procedure forms candidate pairs without subjective/manual assignment. A normal pair relationship provides the reference against which an abnormal relative state may be identified. Such states may reflect M1, M2, M3, or M0. Point-in-time P/S/C/R information may help distinguish or reject mechanisms, predict resolution, and support a trade/no-trade decision. Description, prediction, intervention, and net economic validation remain separate claims.

## Pair-formation philosophy

The formal strategy must be company-aware, fully data-driven, point-in-time, reproducible, and generalizable. Company/business characteristics, factor exposures, empirical co-movement, and relationship stability are candidate information families—not selected factors or frozen formulas. Manual pair assignment is prohibited in the formal strategy.

## Initial falsifiable mechanism hypotheses

- **M1:** delayed peer repricing/information diffusion may imply follower catch-up.
- **M2:** temporary liquidity or flow pressure may imply reversal by the shocked stock.
- **M3:** temporary idiosyncratic relative dislocation may imply relative normalization.
- **M0:** structural, fundamental, ambiguous, or unresolved divergence may make no trade preferable.

The hypotheses must later yield mutually informative, point-in-time observable implications and permit ambiguity or rejection. Operational definitions and tests are deferred.
