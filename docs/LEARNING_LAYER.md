# Learning Layer

The learning layer records how knowledge changes without silently changing the frozen research protocol.

## Objects

- **Question ledger:** unresolved questions linked to decision, assumption, and risk IDs.
- **Hypothesis ledger:** versioned M0–M3 statements, competing explanations, discriminating predictions, and allowed ambiguity.
- **Failure log:** nulls, contradictions, measurement failures, and implementation failures; never deleted.
- **Amendment log:** proposed protocol changes with information already seen, expected impact, and approval outcome.
- **Claim ledger:** each statement tagged DESCRIPTIVE, PREDICTIVE, INTERVENTIONAL, or ECONOMIC and linked to admissible evidence.
- **Negative-learning record:** documents what was ruled out without converting absence of evidence into proof.

## Update discipline

Learning may refine questions at the next permitted gate. It may not rewrite prior decisions, reuse held-out observations for development, or convert exploratory findings into confirmatory evidence. Any post-inspection change creates a new version, is visibly labeled exploratory, and requires fresh untouched validation data before confirmatory use.
