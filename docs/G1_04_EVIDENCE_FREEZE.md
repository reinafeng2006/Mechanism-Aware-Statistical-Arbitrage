# G1-04 — Evidence Freeze

Status: **APPROVED / FROZEN — 2026-09-04**  
Scope: reviewed G1 evidence is frozen as the bounded ancestry for G2 measurement design.

## Approval clarification

G2 is authorized to design measurements for a sequential belief-updating system, not a mandatory M0–M3 classifier. Abnormality, mechanism evidence, and resolution evidence may remain continuous and uncertain. G2 must preserve sequential updating, ambiguity, rejection, and abstention rather than forcing discrete state assignment.

Permission to study or operationalize a construct is not evidence that any formula, estimator, model, threshold, factor or policy should be selected.

## Frozen G1 reopening rule

Broad G1 search/review is closed. Reopen only if: (1) G2 encounters a decision-critical construct lacking adequate ancestry; (2) a previously inaccessible decision-relevant source becomes lawfully available and may change a permitted-use boundary; or (3) later internal work exposes a material identification problem absent from the frozen synthesis. Model underperformance, poor PnL or desire for extra factors is insufficient. Reopening requires a new recorded researcher decision and does not silently alter this freeze.
